# Nexus 0.6.3: next to Local, Herd and Valet, and .local as a domain

## 🤝 Other apps that need ports 80 and 443

Only one app can use ports 80 and 443 at a time. Nexus uses them by default. While Nexus has them, the sites of Local, Herd, Valet, DevKinsta, ServBay, XAMPP or MAMP PRO do not open. Their HTTPS names also show a certificate warning.

- **A notice on the Overview.** When Nexus finds one of these apps on your Mac, the Overview names it and explains the conflict.
- **"Use 8080 and 8443"** gives Nexus other ports in one step. Your sites then open with port 8443 in the URL. The other app can use ports 80 and 443 again.
- **"Stop all services"** frees the ports while you work in the other app.
- The close button hides the notice until Nexus finds another of these apps.

## 🌐 .local as the domain of your sites

Settings > Network > "Domain for sites" now also offers .local. Projects that you move from Local by Flywheel keep their names.

- macOS uses .local for Bonjour. So with .local, Nexus writes each site, alias and workspace into /etc/hosts.
- Subdomains do not work by themselves. Add each one as an alias of its site, for example blog.shop.local.
- Settings, the change alert and the first run explain this before you choose it.

## 🛠 Fixes

- When a port change failed, for example because the new port was in use, the Overview stopped showing why the service did not run. The message now stays until a change works.
