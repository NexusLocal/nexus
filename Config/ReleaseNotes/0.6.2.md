# Nexus 0.6.2: faster removals and a fix for a crash

## 🗑 Remove a workspace without a dump

Before a workspace goes, Nexus writes a dump of each of its databases to the trash. A large database makes that slow. So a removal could take more than 5 minutes.

- **Your choice.** Settings > Workspaces > "Write a dump of each database first" turns the dumps off. A removal is then much faster, but Nexus cannot give the data back.
- **On by default.** Nothing changes until you turn the switch off.
- The alert of a removal, the sheet of old workspaces, `nexus workspace remove` and the MCP server follow the switch.

## 🛠 Fixes

- The background service of Nexus could stop when git added or removed a worktree at the same moment. It then started again by itself, but all your services stopped and started with it.
