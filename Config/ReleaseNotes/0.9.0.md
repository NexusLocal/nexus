# Nexus 0.9.0: the logs of your apps

## 📜 A log viewer

The new Logs screen under Tools shows the log files of the app of each site and workspace. Choose "Show logs" in the menu of a site, or open the screen and choose a site.

- **All logs of a site.** `storage/logs` of Laravel with the daily files, the `debug.log` of WordPress, the PHP errors of the site, the output of a Node.js app and the requests. Craft CMS, Statamic and Symfony work too.
- **Live.** A new entry shows at once.
- **Easy to read.** Each level has its color, each day has its line, and each time is the time of your Mac. An exception shows the line of your app first, and the frames in `vendor/` fold into one row. A click on a file opens your editor at the line.
- **Find it.** Filter by level, search all files at once, or find all entries with the same `trace_id`. Equal entries show as one row with a count. "Hide entries like this" hides the noise.
- **Test runs stay hidden.** A test run writes into the same `laravel.log`. Nexus hides those entries until you want them.
- **Large logs too.** A log of 281 MB opens in a moment.
- **Copy as Markdown.** Give an entry with its exception and its context to a coding agent.
- **Clear log…** empties a file.

## 🌐 In the browser, the terminal and your agent

- **A web page.** The web page of the dumps has a Logs page now. A switch at the top goes from Dumps to Logs.
- **`nexus logs`** shows the newest entries of the site in the current folder. `--level error`, `--search`, `--file` and `--follow` work.
- **Agents.** The MCP tool `read_log` reads the log files of each app, for example only the errors.

## 🛠 Fixes

- **PHP errors with their time.** PHP-FPM wrote the errors of a site without a time. Each PHP version now writes its errors with their time to `php-<version>-errors.log`. The `debug.log` of WordPress still works.
