# Nexus 0.6.1: a safe switch to each new version

## 🔄 Updates no longer lose a change

After an update the background service of Nexus first ends its current work, for example an import or the setup of a workspace. Then it starts the new version. Before 0.6.1 a change in that time could get lost without a message, for example a new setting.

- **The Overview tells you.** A banner says that Nexus starts the new version when its current work ends.
- **No change in the app gets lost.** Until the new version runs, the app takes no change and says why. You can still start and stop services and workspaces.
- **Restart Now** starts the new version at once. It stops the current work.

## 🔕 The dump viewer is off by default

The dump viewer catches nothing until you turn on a Catch switch on the Dumps screen. Until then PHP runs your sites without the dump viewer of Nexus.

- **One time for each user.** The update to 0.6.1 turns all four Catch switches off. This also applies when you turned them on yourself.
- **Turn on what you need.** Use the Dumps screen, or run `nexus dumps settings --catch-dumps on`.

## 🛠 And more

- The wait for the database server of a workspace is more reliable on a busy Mac.
